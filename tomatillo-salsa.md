# Tomatillo Salsa

## Ingredients
- 6 tomatillos, quartered
- 2 serranos, rough chopped
- 2 jalapenos, rough chopped
- 1 medium white onion, quartered
- 6 cloves garlic
- 1/2 bunch of cilantro
- About a tablespoon of salt
- About a teaspoon of Knorr chicken bouillon
- And a splash of water

## Instructions

1. I used a cast iron pot, and got everything charred individually before adding it all to a blender.
2. I did the onion, first followed by the garlic cloves unpeeled in with the peppers, and then I popped the garlic out once they charred on the skin a bit, and I let the cloves brown a little.
3. Last in the cast iron were the tomatillos that I had quartered, and I got them charred as well.
4. Threw it all in my blender as I went, topped it with a half bunch of cilantro, the bouillon and salt and a splash of water.
